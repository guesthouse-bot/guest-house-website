Smart Quotes: how Guest House uses AI for instant staging recommendations

Alex Ryden May 5, 2025

The old way of getting a staging quote

Picture the traditional staging quote process. You call a staging company. They ask for your address and a few details. They schedule a consultation visit—maybe in a few days, maybe next week. A designer drives to your property, walks every room, takes photos, and makes notes. Then they go back to the office, build a proposal, and email it to you a day or two later. From first call to quote in hand: five to ten business days.

Guest House built Smart Quotes to eliminate that friction entirely.

A staging quote should take minutes, not days. Smart Quotes makes that possible without sacrificing accuracy.

What Smart Quotes is

Smart Quotes is Guest House's AI-powered staging recommendation tool. It takes basic information about your property and returns a tailored staging recommendation with pricing in minutes—not days.

Here is what the tool needs from you:

  • The property address
  • Square footage and number of bedrooms/bathrooms
  • Whether the home is vacant or occupied
  • Your listing timeline
  • Any specific concerns or goals (optional)

From that input, Smart Quotes generates a comprehensive recommendation that includes the service level, room-by-room staging plan, estimated cost, and available financing options including pay at close.

How the AI works

Smart Quotes is not a simple calculator that multiplies square footage by a per-foot rate. The underlying model draws on multiple data sources to produce a recommendation that is genuinely tailored to each property:

Property data analysis

Market context

Staging strategy should reflect market conditions. In a strong seller's market, minimal staging may suffice. In a balanced or buyer's market, comprehensive staging becomes more critical. Smart Quotes incorporates current market data for the property's specific submarket—not just the city-wide average, but the neighborhood-level dynamics that influence buyer expectations.

Comparable staging history

Guest House has staged thousands of homes across our markets. Smart Quotes learns from that history. What worked for similar homes? Which rooms generated the most buyer engagement? What staging investment level correlated with the best sale outcomes? This institutional knowledge is encoded in the model.

Service-level matching

Not every home needs the same service. Smart Quotes evaluates whether full-service staging (complete furnishing and design), in-person styling (professional design using the homeowner's existing furnishings), or expert design advice (remote consultation and guidance) is the best fit. The recommendation considers the home's condition, the seller's budget, and what will produce the strongest return for the specific property.

What you get in a Smart Quote

A Smart Quote is not just a price. It is a staging strategy document that gives you and your agent everything needed to make an informed decision:

Recommended service level

The quote specifies whether full-service staging, in-person styling, or design advice is the best fit, with a clear explanation of why.

Room-by-room prioritization

Not every room needs to be staged. Smart Quotes identifies the rooms that will have the greatest impact on buyer perception and sale price. Typically this includes the living room, kitchen, primary bedroom, and one or two secondary spaces, but the specific recommendation adapts to each home.

Estimated pricing

You receive a clear cost estimate for the recommended staging package. No hidden fees, no vague ranges. If pay at close is available, that option is included with a breakdown of how it works.

Timeline expectations

The quote includes an estimated timeline from approval to installation, so you can plan your listing launch accordingly.

How agents are using Smart Quotes

The agents who get the most value from Smart Quotes have integrated the tool into their listing workflow at a specific point: before the listing appointment.

Here is the playbook we see the most successful agents follow:

  1. Before the listing appointment: Run a Smart Quote using the property's public data. This takes two minutes and costs nothing.
  2. During the listing appointment: Present the staging recommendation alongside your marketing plan. The seller sees a specific plan with specific pricing—not a vague suggestion that they should "consider staging."
  3. At the decision point: When the seller asks "how much will staging cost?" you already have the answer. If cost is a concern, present the pay-at-close option. The friction that kills staging decisions—uncertainty and delay—is eliminated.
  4. After approval: Guest House takes over. The Smart Quote becomes the foundation for the staging project, and the design team refines and executes from there.

The agents who win listings are the ones who show up with a plan, not a pitch. Smart Quotes gives you that plan before you walk through the front door.

Accuracy and the human element

A common concern with AI-generated recommendations is accuracy. What if the AI gets it wrong? What if the home has unique characteristics that the model does not account for?

This is where the Smart Quote process differs from pure AI automation. The Smart Quote is a starting point, not the final word. Once you approve the recommendation, a human designer reviews the project, evaluates any nuances the AI may have missed, and refines the plan accordingly. If the designer identifies something the AI did not catch—an unusual layout, custom finishes that need a specific staging approach, or a unique buyer profile—the plan is adjusted.

Think of it as AI handling the 80% that is data-driven and repeatable, while the human designer handles the 20% that requires creativity, judgment, and local knowledge. The combination produces better results than either could alone.

Smart Quotes for homeowners

While agents are the primary users, homeowners can also use Smart Quotes directly. If you are preparing to sell your home and want to understand your staging options before choosing an agent, Smart Quotes gives you an independent, data-informed recommendation.

The broader AI picture

Smart Quotes is one application of AI in the listing prep process, but it fits into a larger trend. AI is making every step of the real estate transaction faster and more data-informed, from pricing to marketing to negotiation. For a broader look at how AI is reshaping listing preparation, read our guide on how AI is changing the way agents prep listings.

The agents and homeowners who embrace these tools early will have a measurable advantage. Not because AI does the work for them, but because it frees them to spend their time on what matters most: the relationship, the strategy, and the details that technology cannot replace.
